# eccofs-model-fields-repo

The scalar fields of NOAA's East Coast Community Ocean Forecast System (ECCOFS),
as gridded data at 0.04° for the oceansensing.org ocean map: sea surface
temperature, salinity and height, the temperature at 50 and 100 m, ocean heat
content (tropical cyclone heat potential), the temperature and salinity at the
bed, the temperature, salinity and vertical velocity at each of 48 depths, the
surface's net heat flux and evaporation less precipitation. Its sibling,
`eccofs-model-currents-repo`, publishes the model's currents.

Updated every three hours.
